whoami?

I am Achilles (aka achilla) and i am 19 years old, i got introduced to cybersecurity through the annual greek ctf competition for highschool students (PMDK), at first it was just a hobby i was indifferent about, but later on, after meeting people of the field and looking into it further with my friends it kind of got stuck in me and i slowly developed a passion, specifically for the pwn category, it was just so fascinating to me to look at low level stuff and how they work under the hood and of course how to break them, from complex exploit chains to tiny bugs only the experienced eye can see it felt kind of natural for me in a way. I am currently a student for the International Hellenic University in Thessaloniki, Greece. I want to one day end up as a top caliber vulnerability researcher. I made this blog to share my challenge write ups, research and notes, from pwn to web exploitation.
